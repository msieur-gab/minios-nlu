# What's playing

Asks the player for the track that is playing. In the PWA there is no player: the answer is an
example; on a device the player's own answer is used.

## Examples
- what's playing{ now| right now|}{?|}
- what is {this|that} {song|track|music}{?|}
- {what|which} {song|track} is {this|that|playing}{?|}
- what's {this|that} {song|track} called{?|}
- what's the name of {this|that} {song|track}{?|}
- who {is this|sings this|plays this|is this by}{?|}
- what am I listening to{?|}
- {name of|title of} {this|the} {song|track}{ please|}
- which album is this{?|}
