# Forget me

Deletes what the agent remembers about you: your name, your place, or both.

## Examples
- forget my name
- forget where I live
- forget my {location|city|home}
- forget {everything|everything about me|all about me|what you know about me}
- {delete|erase|clear|remove} {my name|my location|my city|what you know about me|my data|my details}
- {don't|do not} remember {my name|where I live|me}{ anymore|}
- {stop remembering|unlearn} {my name|where I live|me}
- {reset|wipe} {my profile|what you know|your memory}

## Replies
- name: OK, I've forgotten your name.
- place: OK, I've forgotten where you live.
- all: Done. I don't remember anything about you anymore.
- nothing: There was nothing to forget.
